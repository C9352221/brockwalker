About Me

Brock Walker

A Life Built On Faith & The Right Kind Of Work

Christian·Husband·Brother·Friend·Mortgage Loan Originator·Travel Agent

I am the proud son of Scott & Beverly Walker. They met in high school in Fremont, Ohio. I was born in Sandusky nearby, but we moved to the Ft Lauderdale area at age 1 where my sister Jena was born when I was 5. My parents moved us to Sarasota for my high school years where I attended Cardinal Mooney.

I earned my Bachelor's degree in Business Management from Webber College with a minor in Communications. I also played baseball through college. I moved to Tampa to work at Wells Fargo Financial as a Loan Officer after I graduated. I advanced quickly into management at Wells Fargo & then created a Mortgage company in 2007.

An avid traveler and visiting over 45 countries, I had a life changing experience in Israel inside of Jesus's empty tomb where I accidentally joined an American tour group and created a Christian travel agency shortly after (Star Walker Ministries). I was married to my lovely wife Rafina in 2026.

"What drives me every day is the desire to do God's will."

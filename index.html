About Me

A Life Built on Faith, Family, and the Right Kind of Work

I am the proud son of Scott and Beverly Walker, who met in high school in Fremont, Ohio. I was born in Sandusky and moved to Fort Lauderdale at age one. Sarasota became home in my high school years, and that is where I started to find my own footing.

After earning my Bachelor's degree in Business Management with a minor in Communications, and playing college baseball along the way, I moved to Tampa and started my career at Wells Fargo Financial as a Loan Officer. I advanced quickly into management and shortly after became a mortgage broker, which I have been doing ever since.

"What drives me every day is the desire to do God's will."

That conviction shows up in everything I do, the way I run my mortgage business, the way I lead my Christian travel agency, the way I show up at home, and the way I keep showing up for myself. I want to be the strongest, healthiest, most present version of me, because the people I serve deserve nothing less.

My wife and family come first. My faith comes from the One above all. And my work is simply the place where those two convictions meet the world.
